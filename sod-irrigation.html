Sod, Irrigation & Drainage in Elk Grove & Rancho Cordova

Text Us Call Now

Nothing transforms a yard faster than a fresh sod lawn — green the day it goes down. Green Landscaping installs new sod across Elk Grove, Rancho Cordova and the Sacramento area, with the soil prep, sprinkler coverage and drainage that keep it alive through a Sacramento summer.

We remove the old lawn or weeds, grade and amend the soil, set the sprinkler or drip system to cover every corner, and lay the sod tight. If your yard turns into a mud pit every winter, we build the drainage — French drains, dry creek beds, catch basins — that moves the water out.

  • Old lawn removal, grading and soil prep
  • Fresh sod laid tight and rolled
  • Sprinkler and drip irrigation systems
  • French drains, catch basins & dry creek beds
  • Mulch, planting and finishing touches
