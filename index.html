Turf Done Right Is a System

  • Compacted native base — graded and compacted so it never shifts
  • Crushed rock layer — compacted for structure and drainage
  • Infill sand — a smooth, stable setting bed
  • Drainage backing — water flows through, no puddles
  • Premium artificial turf — cut tight, seamed clean, edged sharp

Cheap turf jobs skip the base work — then wrinkle, flood and fail in two summers. Ours don't.
